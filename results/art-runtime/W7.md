## 1. How the measurement was made (no ptrace by default)

`yama ptrace_scope=1` and the guest is not our descendant. A **scratch** rebuild of the
sysroot gralloc shim (`gralloc_shim.c` + `research/art-feasibility/w3-leash/scripts/ptracer.c`)
was used for two additions only: `PR_SET_PTRACER(-1)` and, gated on the already-forwarded
`E3G_DEBUG`, a self-`tgkill(SIGSTOP)` (`kill`/`tkill` are seccomp-denied in the guest;
`tgkill` is allowed). The guest therefore stops before Java starts, gdb attaches and sets
breakpoints, then `continue` runs the whole launch. **The original sysroot shim was
restored byte-for-byte from `.cache/w7/libacoshw.so.orig`; `gralloc_shim.c` is unchanged.**

Because the binary is stripped, breakpoints were set at `libgui.so` base + `nm -D` offsets;
the base is the offset-0 mapping of `/proc/<pid>/maps`. Every logged row below comes from
that guest (`.cache/w7/bq.log`).

## 2. Measured: four complete frames, the fifth dequeue is the wait

Thread 1 (the app main thread) executes, in order, four times:

```text
Surface::lock → Surface::dequeueBuffer → BQP::dequeueBuffer → BQP::requestBuffer
  → Surface::unlockAndPost → Surface::queueBuffer → BQP::queueBuffer
  → BLASTBufferQueue::onFrameAvailable → acquireNextBufferLocked
  → BufferQueueConsumer::acquireBuffer
```

then a fifth `Surface::lock → dequeueBuffer` that never returns. Queue fields at every
entry (core + `0x1344`, `0x1348`, `0x134c`, `0x1330`, `0x1370`, `0x13a4`, `0x13b4`,
`0x13b5`):

```text
mMaxBufferCount=64  mMaxAcquiredBufferCount=2  mMaxDequeuedBufferCount=2
mAllowAllocation=1  mIsAllocating=0  mAsyncMode=0  mDequeueBufferCannotBlock=0
```

`getMaxBufferCountLocked()` = min(64, 2+2) = **4**, and the core starts with four free
slots. Four dequeues consume all four; `waitForFreeSlotThenRelock` then waits on
`mDequeueCondition` with BLAST's `setDequeueTimeout(max)` (the blocked PLT slot resolves to
`std::__1::condition_variable::__do_timed_wait`, GOT `0x192c80`). The kernel-level futex
`0x89` W6 measured is this wait — not the first dequeue.

Two `BLASTBufferQueue`s are constructed (both `maxDequeued=2`, `maxAcquired=2`): the first
never dequeues, the second is the app window.

## 3. Why the slots are not released: no reverse callback ever reaches the guest

Breakpoints on the whole reverse path are **never hit** in a 20 s launch:

- `TransactionCompletedListener::onTransactionCompleted` (`0xe4d30`)
- `BnTransactionCompletedListener::onTransact` (`0x12d520`)
- `BLASTBufferQueue::transactionCommittedCallback` (`0xcda10`)
- `TransactionCompletedListener::onReleaseBuffer` (`0xbf330`)

BLAST acquires a buffer, sends the buffer transaction, and waits for the commit callback
before releasing; because none arrives, the acquired buffers stay acquired, the queue
fills, and the producer's fifth dequeue waits. This is the "no consumer acquires/releases"
alternative W6 listed, now measured.

## 4. The chronologically first error: `BufferData` decode of the real parcel

The peer rejects every BLAST transaction (`.cache/w7/run8.out`, then `.cache/w7/run9.out`):

```text
acos-binder-peer: UNSUPPORTED_ANDROID_API {"code":8,"descriptor":"android.ui.ISurfaceComposer",
 "error":"SERVICE_INTERNAL", ... "message":"internal service error:
 android.gui.ISurfaceComposerClient: parcel: object at 108 is not a binder (type 0x00000013)"}
```

With `ACOS_W5_DUMP=1` the two `FrameTimelineInfo` interpretations behave as W5 documented
(`skip=24` is the image's; `skip=28` is 4 bytes late), and `skip=24` advances through the
layer state to the end of the 700-byte parcel:

```text
ACOSDBG timeline skip=24 size_word=28 failed at 700: ... parcel underflow: need 4 byte(s) at 700, have 0
ACOSDBG ls ... trusted at 564
ACOSDBG ls hasbuf=true at 568
ACOSDBG bd flags at 580 hasbuf=true
ACOSDBG gb header len=1935813253 fdc=19 at 588
```

`1935813253 = 0x73622a85 = BINDER_TYPE_BINDER`: at the position where r75 `BufferData`
writes `bool hasBuffer` + `GraphicBuffer::flatten` (`len=88, fds=1`), the image's bytes are
an object reference. The parser then reads a 1.9-billion-word header and underflows. The
exact image layout of `BufferData`'s buffer / cached-buffer / fence fields is the next
wall; it must be established the W3 way (byte replay against the image's writer), not
guessed.
