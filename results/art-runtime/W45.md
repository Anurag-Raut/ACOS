## 8. Reconciliation with W41 (review response)

### 8.1 Why "primary − job-scope formula ≈ −0.0008" did not mean "guest ≈ 0.5 %"

The two numbers are **different metric types**:

* `primary` = `memory.stat` **anon+shmem** of job **+ guest** (page cache excluded);
* `scope_runner_formula` = `memory.current` of the **job scope only**, which is
  `job anon+shmem + job file cache + job kernel/sock`.

So `primary − scope = guest anon+shmem − job file/kernel`. For T07 good these two
happen to be almost equal, which is why the totals nearly coincided. Decomposed
(reconcile run + the sibling suite run):

| component | GiB·min | source |
|---|---:|---|
| job anon+shmem | 0.00339 | reconcile run, job cgroup `memory.stat` |
| guest anon+shmem | **0.00515** | reconcile run, guest cgroup `memory.stat` (**58.6 %** of combined 0.00879) |
| job file + kernel | ≈ 0.0052 | job `memory.current` 0.00856 − job anon+shmem 0.00339 (suite T07-good, r2 round 1) |
| combined anon+shmem (raw) | 0.00879 | job + guest |

The near-equality is `job file ≈ guest anon+shmem`. After the same-type sampler fix
the aggregate primary (0.1622) is 10.3 % **above** the old formula (0.1471), so the
red flag is gone at suite level too.

### 8.4 Sampler exclusion: evidence, and the defect the review found

* **It really is in the job scope.** `sample_scope` records its own
  `/proc/self/cgroup`; `self_cgroup_equals_job: true` in the reconcile run (the process
  `guarded.sh` starts is `benchlib.py sample-scope`; `acos verify` is its child).
* **What was subtracted (before the review):** full `VmRSS` (20.6 → 22.0 MiB in the
  reconcile run). But primary is **anon+shmem**; VmRSS also contains file-backed pages
  (a sampler-like interpreter after importing benchlib: VmRSS 19.62, **RssAnon 7.43**,
  RssFile 12.19 MiB — 38 % anon). Subtracting VmRSS from an anon+shmem integral
  over-subtracts and **inflates** M11. Corrected in this review: subtract
  `RssAnon+RssShmem`; keep full-RSS as a reported sensitivity.
* **Effect:** on the same suite code, M11 74.31 (full-RSS subtraction) → **67.81**
  (same-type subtraction). Sensitivities in §3.2: no exclusion 62.12, full-RSS 74.92.
* **Test with teeth:** `test_sampler_cost_excluded` asserts the subtracted amount is
  the anon+shmem figure, that the full-RSS figure is reported but not subtracted, and
  that the same-type charge is smaller; `test_anon_shmem_probe_uses_rss_split` checks
  the probe reads RssAnon+RssShmem rather than VmRSS.
