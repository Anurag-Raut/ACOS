   is a test-only override. Do not widen it.

   hostile `setTransactionState`/`sizeCompatScale` parcels. §5 #6 asks for both
   in one live APK; the decoder side remains the S7 `sandbox_c1` corpus.

   labelled and never independent evidence.
4. Logs/markers are guest-writable and labelled guest-asserted.
5. `text_ocr` is `UNSUPPORTED_ASSERTION`; no OCR engine was added.
6. Binder transactions carry a host-observed provenance label but are not yet a
   per-step artifact.
7. Adjacent files outside the S8 OWN list were edited because the OUTCOME cannot
   be implemented without them: `crates/acos-protocol/src/{error,assertion,scenario}.rs`,
   its generated schemas, the stale scenario-count test, and
