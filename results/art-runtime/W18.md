### 3.1 View hierarchy (ids, classes, texts)

phone at 400 dpi, and the phone's app window starts at display y=108 (its own
status bar). Phone-space value = `x_dp` edges scaled by 400/420 and shifted by
+108 on y; **full-width edges (0 and 1080 px) are not scaled** (both displays
are 1080 px wide, so a `match_parent` edge stays at 1080 — only dp-derived
positions/sizes are scaled).

### 3.5 What a verification agent would be **misled by**

* **Tree text vs rendered glyphs disagree** on the live path: `observe --tree`
  says `Increment`, the C1 frame shows `INCREMENT`. An exact-text assertion
  passes while a pixel/VLM check of the same frame sees different glyphs. Any
  screenshot-based check of a Material button on the live ART path must tolerate
  all-caps until #2 is fixed.
* **The title is the component name**, so "the app title matches the manifest
  label" checks fail on the live path but pass on the host renderer and on the
  phone. A title assertion on ACOS live evidence would be a false negative
  against the phone, and a `--reference` snapshot taken from the host renderer
  would hide the live discrepancy.
* **Frames are window-sized (1080×2209), phone screenshots are display-sized
  (1080×2400).** Naive top-left alignment (no +108 offset) shifts every element
  by the status-bar height and makes a correct layout look 108 px off; naive
  scaling of full-width edges (×400/420) turns a `match_parent` edge into 1029
  and looks like a 51 px width bug. Both mistakes were observed while producing
  §3.1.
* **Small vertical deltas (+3 px) are real but minor** — do not read them as a
  layout regression; they come from the count `TextView` line height.
* The **83 px missing bottom** is an inset difference, not a clipped layout: the
  ACOS window simply does not include the phone's navigation area.
