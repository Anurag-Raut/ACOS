### 2.3 One ART run per lock acquisition; load recorded per rep

Every sample/cohort-rep/suite-task is its own
`run-exclusive.sh --task W33 -- guarded.sh --mem 8G --time 900 -- <one ART run>`,
so W30/W31/W32 ART work interleaved. The cost is visible in the record: per-rep
wall up to 274 s (queue) and `loadavg1` 0.6 → **16.81** while MemAvailable stayed
43.1–43.8 GiB. Samples whose host gate failed (loadavg1 > 4) are marked
`contaminated` and are excluded from verdicts (a contaminated sample can never
PASS); M2's 5/5 N=5 reps and 7/10 M9 reps are so marked and the row says so.
