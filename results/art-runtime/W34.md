### 1.2 The single dominant item, before the fix

`wait_for` never overshoots (11–22 ms total). The cost was **one input class**:

| variant | step | before3 | after8 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| T07-form-good | tap (name field, focus-only) | 16 059 ms | 4 7xx ms |
| T07-form-good | tap (submit) | 17 432 ms | 2 0xx ms |
| T07-form-wrong | tap + tap | 16 111 + 17 430 ms | 4 7xx + 2 0xx ms |
| T08-persist-good | tap (first field) | 16 087 ms | 4 7xx ms |
| T10-isolation-good | tap (first field) | 16 077 ms | 4 7xx ms |
| T12-dialog-good | tap | 17 118 ms | 5 0xx ms |

Root cause (measured, not hypothesised): `acos-art`'s input reply settles only
when a **new C1 buffer generation**, a **new probe sample** *and* **changed
pixels** are all true inside `ACOSLAUNCH_FRAME_WAIT_SECS` (15 s). A focus-only
tap changes no pixels (`frames/1.png` and `frames/3.png` of T07-good were
byte-identical, md5 `001a6665b419`), so the guest answered `NO_FRESH_FRAME`
after the *whole* window and the engine then took one recovery capture
(≈1 s): 16 s for an action that changed nothing. The submit tap in the same
app changes the view tree but not the pixels, and also burned 15 s.

## 6. Residuals (measured, not guessed)

1. **Guest-side settle per settling tap**: `tap` is still 84.6 s of 132.5 s
   (63.9 %). A tap that the guest settles costs 1.9–2.4 s (generation-change →
   quiet 150 ms → probe sample after it), a recovered one 4.7–5.0 s
   (3 s guest window + 2 × 1.2 s captures). Owner: `crates/acos-art` (W31/W33).
2. **`assert_log` wait** 10.5 s (7.9 % of the suite): each wrong twin whose line
   never appears pays its scenario `timeout_ms` (5 s) *before* the step timer
   starts. Legitimately a wait; the attribution fix is a request (§3).
3. **Guest boot** 0.55 s/variant and **shutdown** 0.16 s/variant are now
   measured; both are outside the verdict clock, so M11's wall component ignores
   them and the suite's `wall_ms` understates process wall by ~13 %.
4. **`reset`** 2.1–2.3 s total (T09 only, scenario asks for it) — a real relaunch
   in a fresh process, kept.
5. **`type`** 6.5 s (≈0.55 s per type step) — one input round trip per string;
   not reducible without batching in the guest.
6. Assertions cost nothing (`assert_text` 75 ms total): they read the bound
   frame. `assert_region` 1.26 s total (PNG decode + compare, needed).
7. **M11 denominator noise** (4.01 vs 2.37 GiB·min for one binary) — the
   guarded-scope `memory.current` integral is host-contaminated; a per-job
   `systemd-run --user --scope` (as `run-matrix.sh` does) is needed before any
   M11 verdict is published.

## 7. Design note (requested, not implemented): warm guest pool (advisor item 7)

**Measured inputs (this task, N=1, quiet window, 22 variants):**

| quantity | value | source |
|---|---:|---|
| guest boot (`acos-art` spawn → ready) | **430–560 ms, mean 549 ms** (12 083 ms / 22) | `art-frames/session-timing.json` |
| first `tree` after ready (`launch` step) | 481 ms mean (10 593 / 22) | `verdict.steps[0]` |
| quiet shutdown (`close` + group proof) | 157 ms mean (3 451 / 22) | `session-timing.json` |
| per-variant verdict wall (after the fix) | 5 310 ms mean (116 819 / 22) | `suite-W34-after8.json` |
| per-variant `job_gib_min` | 0.088 mean (1.937 / 22) | same |
| cgroup `memory.current` peak of a guarded suite run | 321–1 591 MiB, monotone across variants | same (`cgroup_peak_mib`) |

**What a pool could remove:** the boot plus the first post-boot traversal, i.e.
≈1.0 s of a 5.3 s verdict wall and ≈1.2 s of the 6.0 s process wall — 19 % of
process wall, **~9 % of M11's wall component**.

**What it costs:** M11 charges pool idle time to the jobs it serves. Two spare
guests at ~0.5–0.9 GiB held for a 2-minute suite add **≈1.0–1.8 GiB·min** to the
current `job_gib_min` of 1.94 — that alone moves M11 from 5.7 to ~2.9, i.e. the
pool makes M11 **worse** at N=1 even if boot became free. It only pays when the
boot is amortised over ≥N concurrent jobs whose own boot would otherwise be
serialised: at N=10 the same two spares serve 10 jobs and the per-job charge is
0.2 GiB·min against a 1.0 s wall saving — that is the regime to measure, not N=1.

**Recommendation:** do not build the pool for the N=1 correctness suite. Rank it
below (a) the guest-side input settle (§6.1, 84.6 s of 132.5 s) and (b) fixing
the M11 denominator (§6.7), because a pool cannot be shown to pay for itself
until the denominator is measurable. If it is built for N=10, gate it on
M11(with pool) ≥ M11(without) per advisor item 7 and charge the spares to the
jobs they serve, sampled per job cgroup as item 3 requires.
