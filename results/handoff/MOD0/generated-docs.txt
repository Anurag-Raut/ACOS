---LIMITS-MARKDOWN---
### Service modules (generated from the module registry)

A module is attached with `acos_launch {services: [{id, profile}]}` or the `--service id[:profile]` CLI flag. `off` means the framework takes its real "missing" path and every reply says so. `stub` and `scripted` answers are never a real implementation and a verdict that relies on them says so.

| module | profiles | attach | RAM | what it provides |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `core` | `off`, `real` | at-launch | ≤16 MiB | `activity`, `window`, `activity_task`, `package`, `display`, `accessibility`, `autofill`, `textservices`, `audio`, `graphicsstats`, `network_management`, `game`, `virtualdevice`, `content_capture`, `voiceinteraction`, `content`, `settings`, `input`, `input_method`, `ime_session`, `ime_tracker`, `clipboard`, `user`, `uimode`, `notification`, `statusbar`, `permission`, `appops`, `connectivity`, `androidfuture`, `SurfaceFlinger`, `sensorservice`, `mount`, `shortcut`, `jobscheduler`, `power`, `account`, `thermalservice`, `alarm`, `location`, `trust`, `restrictions`, provider `settings` |
| `feedback.vibrator` | `off`, `stub`, `scripted` | hot | ≤1 MiB | `vibrator_manager` |
