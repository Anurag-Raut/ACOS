**Readings.**
* **Latency is flat in N.** On the animations-on arm the per-task wall is 1422–1460 ms at
  N=1…20 (p90 1444–1502), i.e. within ±1.3 % of the N=1 number; off is 496–544 ms
  (p90 517–575). Concurrency adds throughput, not latency: 0.65 → 2.68 → 4.96 → 7.41
  verified tasks/s on (13.9× at N=20), 1.82 → 6.68 → 11.2 → 10.5 off.
* **The per-session footprint is flat in N** — 87/77/73/73 MiB anon+shmem peak per session
  (N=1 carries the whole boot-image share), 65 MiB on the off arm at N≥10. ΣPss/session
  *falls* with N (177 → 74 → 62 → 53 MiB) because Pss divides the shared framework/boot
  image by its mapping count; 65.6 MiB/session cgroup at N=10 is the label-matched number
  from E3.2 and the two are consistent.
* **Verified rate: 1173/1176 tasks (99.74 %) over all 24 N≤20 cells**, 0 false passes. The
  only losses are two reset steps in the on arm at N=10 (1/150) and N=20 (2/300); §6.
* **The animations-off arm is 2.7–2.9× lower latency at every N** (496→544 ms vs
  1422→1460 ms), i.e. the same 2.7–2.9× on steady throughput per GiB. E3.3's larger
  off-arm GiB-min ratio came from the biased denominator (`S3c`). It stays a mode, not a
  default.
* **Concurrency buys throughput almost linearly**: 0.65 → 7.41 tasks/s on (11.4× at N=20),
  1.82 → 10.5 off (5.8× — the off arm is already reset/launch-bound at N≤5).

### 1.2 The GiB denominator (measured, and why the derived number is also given)

The bench reports two denominators, both integrals over the measured cohort sampled at
10 Hz:

* **cgroup** (`memory.current`, page cache included). At N=1 it is 278 GiB-min⁻¹; at N=5 it
  jumps to 663 because the cohort now amortises the boot transient over 5 sessions. It is
  the historical headline and it is **not comparable across acquisitions**: it is dominated
  by page cache charged to whichever cgroup first read the image (E3.2 §4b), which is why
  the off arm's fresh acquisitions report 775–1704 here while E3.2's loop-only arm reported
  5239 for the same kernel work.
* **anon+shmem** (label-matched with M1/M2). On the animations-on arm it moves 888 → 848 →
  721 → 603 with N, i.e. it *falls* as sessions amortise the shared boot image.

† **The off-arm anon+shmem numbers (1414–10 890) are inflated and must not be quoted as a
record.** They are the same P5 integral, but the sampler discovers the guests' scopes on a
1 s cadence (`WarmSampler.discover` every 10 ticks) while an animations-off task's guest
lives ~0.5 s, so most samples see only the daemon's own ~20 MiB. The bias is proportional
to how short-lived the guests are: it inflates the short-cohort/off arm ~5–10× and the
on arm ~1.4× (44 MiB average vs the 65 MiB the same sampler peaks at). Recorded as `S3c`.

Because of that, the table also gives the denominator that does not depend on sampling:
**at the measured 65.6 MiB/session (E3.2 M2, N=10) the steady-state rate is
`60 / (p50_s × 0.0641)` = 651 / 658 / 648 / 641 tasks/GiB-min at N=1/5/10/20 (on) and
1886 / 1834 / 1804 / 1723 (off)** — flat in N, by construction, because latency and
per-session footprint are both flat in N. That is the honest Stage-3 claim: **scale buys
throughput linearly and costs linearly; verified tasks per GiB is a constant of the loop
shape, not of N.**

## 4. Gaten at scale (RN 0.86.3 / Fabric / Hermes, real ART)

Method: `tools/bench/s3_scale.py --mode gaten`; `--n` sessions launched together behind a
thread barrier, `acos_launch {settle:"fast", clear_state:true, animations:"on"}` (fast
observation, no strict assert — Gaten's welcome screen never strictly settles; GR3 owns
that), then a fast tree read, then 2 s later a second tree read (the immediate read sees
only the RN root), then all sessions held 5 s for the memory read, then destroy.
`session-exit.json` is scanned for crash evidence (R3).

* **16/16 launches reached first frame and a real tree**, 2 reps per N. No crash, no guest
  signal, no exit error: every retained `session-exit.json` says `outcome: ok`,

## 6. Defects found (evidence + Backlog rows)

Both are in the **reset → relaunch** path at N≥10 on the animations-on arm; both are
stochastic (~0.2–1 % of tasks in a cell) and both leave the session unusable for the next task.

**S3a — reset relaunch can hang with no live frame (60 s).**
`acos_reset` → relaunch → `NO_FRESH_FRAME` / `LIVE_FRAME_UNAVAILABLE`, 60 048 ms:
`no live frame within 60s: activity_resumed=true probe=true c1_generation=0`
(`crates/acos-art/src/launch.rs` startup loop, the `startup_budget` expiry path).
The guest resumed its Activity and the in-guest probe produced tree samples, but C1 never
stored a frame (`c1_generation` stayed 0), so the launcher killed it after the 60 s budget.
Seen at N=20 (bench rep r2-on, 1/100 tasks) and reproduced independently (1/100, repro2 r2);
one N=20 off rep also has a 60 s *successful* reset that inflated its cohort wall.
Evidence: `results/bench/S3-evidence/loop-n20-reset-nofreshframe.json` (the failing task,
the `activity-resumed.marker`, `application-created.marker` and `session-timing.json` of
`p5_n20-a3`).

**S3b — reset relaunch guest exits 1 almost immediately.**
`GUEST_EXITED: the guest ended before its first live frame (status exit status: 1)` after
133–301 ms. Seen at N=10 (1/150 tasks) and N=20 (1/300) in the same bench rep, and
reproduced (1/100, repro r3). No signal, no `native_crash` record; the launcher's guard
line for that class was not retained in the evidence run (R3's `native_crash` retention
only fires on a crash signal), so the cause is **not** identified — it is the same symptom
class H17 fixed (torn guard plan / mapped APK), which must be ruled out with the guard line
next time. Evidence: `results/bench/S3-evidence/loop-n20-reset-guestexited.json`.

**S3c — the bench's anon+shmem integral and Pss sampler undercount churn (tool defect).**
`WarmSampler` rediscovers guest cgroups every 1 s; a guest that lives < 1 s (every
animations-off task, every short compat app) is missing from most samples, so the
anon+shmem GiB-min integral is inflated 5–10× on the off arm and ~1.4× on the on arm (the
`†` column). `session_pss()` sums only the serve's *current* children, so during a relaunch
burst many children are launchers without their guest yet: the off N=20 reps read
2.7 / 0.0 / 51.5 MiB per session for the same 65 MiB/session workload. The per-session
*peak* anon+shmem (`session_pss_peak`, `anon_shmem_peak_per_session`) is the stable number
and is what §1's memory column states.

## 7. Limits

* The two reset defects are load-correlated in the sample but not proven to be caused by
  load: both were seen at load1 1.1–3.5, and the 60 s hang reproduced at load1 8.8. They are
  stochastic; the packet's rates (1/150, 2/300, 1/100) are small-sample.
* One N=10 animations-off rep (519 → 770 ms p50 / 1193 p90) is a host-load straggler
  (load1 2.4, four other agents); it is kept in the per-rep table, not deleted.
* N=30 is the largest N measured; the "ceiling" is therefore "≥ N=30", not a proved maximum.
* Compat ran with `settle: fast` and a 25 s first-frame budget (K1/K3 used the strict ladder
  and 20 s); the first-frame rung is comparable (launch returns at the frame in both), the
  later ladder rungs (tap/screenshot) were not re-run at scale here.
* Gaten's welcome screen still does not strictly settle (GR3); S3 measured it with fast
  observation by design, so this record says nothing about strict-settle behaviour.
