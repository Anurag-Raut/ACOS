## 5. Open risks

* The positive after arm was load-contaminated (load1 6.76, `noisy=true`); the comparison is
  still inside noise but a quiet re-run is preferable for a headline number.
* `--m6-apk` is a diagnostic knob for negative controls; it is not part of any product path.
* `ChurnSampler` costs ~2.3–2.6 % of one core in these N=5 cohorts (8 % at N=20 in S3c); at N=30
  the cost grows with live scopes.
* Capping PNGs keeps the newest 20 samples per harness; a failure investigation may need
  `--keep-screens` raised for that run (documented in the help text).
